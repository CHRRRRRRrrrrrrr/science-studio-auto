Science Studio Auto

A personal desktop tool for creating short science-explainer videos.

Science Studio Auto is a desktop application, used by its creator, that turns public astronomy research abstracts into short vertical explainer videos: it writes a plain-language script, adds a narrated voice-over, subtitles and public NASA imagery, and exports a ready-to-post video file.

When the creator connects their own TikTok account, the application can send the finished video to that same account as a draft or post, always with the "AI-generated content" label enabled.
