Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Science Studio Auto ("the App") is a desktop application that runs only on the computer of its user. Contact: sciencecommunautaire@gmail.com.

2. What the App does with your data

The App runs locally. It does not operate a server and does not collect, store or sell personal data on any server of ours.

  • TikTok account connection. If you choose to connect your TikTok account, TikTok provides an access token to the App. This token is stored only in a configuration file on your own computer and is used only to upload or publish the videos that you create with the App to your own account.
  • Videos and scripts. Videos, scripts and captions are created and saved on your computer. They are sent to TikTok only when you or your scheduled task upload them.
  • Third-party services. To create a video, the App sends public research abstracts (from arXiv) to a text-generation service (OpenAI) to write the script, uses Microsoft's text-to-speech for the voice, and retrieves public images from the NASA Image and Video Library. No TikTok account data is sent to these services.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, browsing history, location or any TikTok profile data beyond what is needed to complete an upload for the connected account.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

All data stays on your computer. You can remove the App's access at any time by deleting its configuration file and by revoking the App in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites). Uninstalling or deleting the App removes all locally stored data.

6. Children

The App is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
